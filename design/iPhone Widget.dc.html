iPhone widgets
The app (middle) runs the full turntable: spinning record, pet walking to the arm, record swaps and the crate. Tap “Continue with Spotify” to walk through sign-in. On the home screen, tap play, the record, or skip on the medium widget: the arm drops, the record spins, the pet dances, and skipping slides the record out and a new one in.
New: tap the large widget’s sleeve to use your own album art. The widget colours, progress bar and Live Activity pick up the cover’s colour, and the cover is shared with the Mac widget. Drag the large widget’s progress bar to scrub. Theme and sound are in Tweaks.
iOS home-screen widgets render as snapshots, so the record can’t spin continuously and the pet can’t walk. State changes (arm down, pet pose, new cover) animate as transitions between snapshots. Full motion would live in the app or a Live Activity.
